# News images

Created with the built-in image-generation tool for the three News cards. These are illustrative generated photos, not photographs of actual customers or properties.

Files: `small-business.png`, `online-claims.png`, `business-property.png`.

Each prompt used this template, substituting the subject below:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create a landscape editorial photograph for an insurance website news card, wide 3:2 composition. Subject: [subject]. Natural daylight, professional trustworthy atmosphere, realistic details, restrained blue and neutral palette. Main subject centrally framed to remain visible in a shallow 2:1 crop. No text, logos, watermarks, collage, or added graphic overlays.

- Small business: A small business owner reviewing an inventory checklist at the counter of a neat independent shop, shelves softly visible behind them
- Online claims: Close-up over-the-shoulder view of a person using a laptop at a tidy desk to submit an insurance claim, a document folder beside it, screen displaying a simple generic form with no readable text or branding
